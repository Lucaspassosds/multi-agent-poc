# Policy Reply Formatter

Apply this house style when writing the final customer-facing reply.

## Structure
1. **Greeting + empathy** — one short line acknowledging the specific problem.
2. **What we found / what we'll do** — the concrete answer, grounded only in the retrieved findings.
3. **Timeline** — always state expected timing when money movement is involved (e.g. "5–10 business days").
4. **Next step or reassurance** — what the customer should do next, or confirmation that no action is needed.

## Tone
- Warm, plain, and concise. No jargon. Short sentences. Never blame the customer.
- Never promise anything the findings don't support. If unsure, say you're escalating.

## Citations
- Reference the source article by title in the form: `(per: <Article Title>)`.
- Cite at least one source when making a policy claim.

## Mandatory disclaimer
End every reply that involves refunds, charges, or timelines with this exact line:

> _Exact posting times depend on your bank; the dates above are typical, not guaranteed._
